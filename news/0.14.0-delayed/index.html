Good News! 0.14.0 Delayed

February 14, 2025

Hello everyone,

Happy Valentine’s Day. You are all my valentines.

To quote myself:

I am now setting a hard deadline…

Statements dreamed up by the utterly deranged.

To keep it brief - planned 0.14.0 release date is moved to Monday, March 3rd, three days after HYTRADBOI.

Mainly, there’s a good chance Jacob will have progressed the x86_64 backend far enough that we can enable it by default for Debug mode on ELF targets.

Secondly, there are some important pull requests in the queue that I didn’t get to yet.

Thanks all - your patience will be rewarded with ~4x faster compilation and better debugging experience.
